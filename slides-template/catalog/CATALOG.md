- **Font:** Montserrat
- **File mẫu:** `slides-template/output/Mau-Bao-Cao-Lime.pptx` (7.4 MB)
- **Dựng:** `python build_deck.py --theme lime --content <file.json> --name <Ten-file>` — nội dung mẫu: `catalog/samples/M01-M02-baocao.json`
